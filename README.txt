MCU INTERACTIVE CATCH-UP

Open index.html in a web browser. Keep the assets folder beside it. The site works offline with its included images.

Use the arrow keys, Previous/Next buttons, or slide list to move through the 116 story pages. Your place is saved in this browser. Hover over highlighted names and terms for a quick reminder. Click a name or a portrait in the People Here strip for a character profile, with universe, team, current status, appearances, and linked story beats.

The Explore menu opens the Character Directory, Universe Map, Story Paths, and Films & Shows library. The library has 58 relevant recaps; each title links its characters and story pages, with Previous/Next controls for reading through the titles. The Glossary explains recurring multiverse terms.

The X-Men films and Deadpool share Earth-10005 here. The Days of Future Past change is shown as a branch within that world; Earth-838 is a separate Xavier variant.

This is a companion to the PowerPoint. It includes 103 story or film images; other pages use diagrams, a roster, or cast portraits. The Endgame Encore TVA readout is a labeled illustration because a verified still was not available. Captions identify the film or series shown when a related image illustrates a story beat.
